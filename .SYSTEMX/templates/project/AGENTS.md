Read [START-HERE.md](START-HERE.md). Preserve exact .SYSTEMXP spelling.

Use its local Agent 0 for coordination and acceptance. A role record does not
launch a worker or grant external access. Environment and user instructions
remain authoritative.

allowed files, dependencies, acceptance criteria, and reporting destination.
